About Property Claims Loss

An insurance appraisal firm focused on one thing: resolving disagreements over the amount of a property loss fairly and on the record.

Who we are

Property Claims Loss Inc. is an insurance appraisal firm based in Chicago, Illinois. With a complete staff of qualified and licensed appraisers and umpires, we provide appraiser services and umpire services for residential and commercial property claims, using the appraisal clause in the insurance policy to resolve disputes over the amount of loss without litigation.

What we believe

  • The number should be documented. Every figure we present is backed by inspection, measurement, photos and line-item pricing.
  • Appraisal is not litigation. The goal is an accurate amount of loss, reached efficiently, not a prolonged fight.
  • Roles must stay clean. As an appraiser we present our client's position honestly. As an umpire we are neutral. We never mix the two on one claim.
  • Plain language. Policyholders deserve to understand the process, the costs and the likely outcomes before they commit.

Credentials on staff

Property Claims Loss has a complete staff of qualified and licensed appraisers and umpires. Our staff holds appraiser and umpire certifications, adjuster licenses in Iowa, Louisiana, Florida, Alabama, Mississippi, Nevada and Texas, and experience as an Executive General Adjuster.

What we do not do

We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. Appraisal decides the amount of loss, not coverage. If your claim was denied or involves coverage questions, we recommend speaking with a licensed attorney in your state.
