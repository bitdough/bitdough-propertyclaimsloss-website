Email an appraisal request

The button opens a pre-filled email with the details we need.

What to include

  • Your name, phone and the property address
  • Insurance carrier and claim number
  • Date and type of loss (hail, wind, water, fire, other)
  • Whether you need an appraiser or an umpire
  • Whether appraisal has already been demanded, and by whom
  • The carrier's estimate and your estimate or contractor bid

For umpire assignments, please copy both appraisers so we can run a conflict check and keep communications equal.
